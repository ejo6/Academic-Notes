## General Regions
In [[1. Double Integrals]] in we looked at double integrals over basic rectangular regions. Most regions are not rectangular so we need to expand the base.
$$
\iint \limits_D f(x,y)\ dA
$$
Where $D$ is any region. There are 2 types of base regions to consider, horizontal bounds and vertical bounds.
![This is the 2D graph of two unknown functions on the domain a<x<b that are in the 1st quadrant.  The graph of $y=g_{2}(x)$ is always over the graph of $y=g_{1}(x)$. Use set builder notation for these regions, here are both cases respectively: 
$$
D=\{(x,y)\}|a\leq x \leq b,\  g_1(x)\leq y \leq g_2(x)\}
$$
$$
D=\{(x,y)\}|h_1(x)\leq x \leq h_2(x),\ c\leq y \leq d\}
$$
This basically means that use all x values between $a$ and $b$, and $y$ between $g_1(x)$ and $g_2(x)$. Same thing with case 2: use all $y$ values between $c$ and $d$, and $x$ between $h_1(y)$ and $h_2(y)$.

The double integral bounds are defined similar to the rectangle, in case 1 when $D=\{(x,y)\}|a\leq x \leq b,\  g_1(x)\leq y \leq g_2(x)\}$ the integral is,
$$
\iint \limits_D f(x,y)\ dA=\int_a^b \int_{g_1(x)}^{g_2(x)}f(x,y)\ dy\ dx
$$
In case 1 when $D=\{(x,y)\}|h_1(x)\leq x \leq h_2(x),\ c\leq y \leq d\}$ the integral is,
$$
\iint \limits_D f(x,y)\ dA=\int_c^d \int_{h_1(y)}^{h_2(y)}f(x,y)\ dx\ dy
$$
### Properties to Recall and Apply
$$
\text{1.} \iint \limits_D f(x,y) + g(x,y) \ dA = \iint \limits_D f(x,y)\ dA + \iint \limits_D g(x,y)\ dA
$$
For any constant $c$ 
$$
\text{2.} \iint \limits_D cf(x,y) \ dA = c\iint \limits_D f(x,y)\ dA
$$
If the region $D$ can be split into two regions $D_1$ and $D_2$ then the integral can be broken down to
$$
\text{3.} \iint \limits_D f(x,y) \ dA = \iint \limits_{D_1} f(x,y)\ dA + \iint \limits_{D_2} f(x,y)\ dA
$$
